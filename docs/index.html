About ZTS Telecom

Built for Serious Buyers. Trusted by Many.

ZTS Telecom FZ-LLC is a government-licensed mobile phone trading company operating out of the Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ) Freezone, UAE. We specialize in sourcing, trading, and delivering brand new and certified pre-owned smartphones from every major brand.

With deep roots in the UAE telecom market and strong supply chains reaching across Pakistan, we bridge two markets with trust, transparency, and speed.

Government Licensed

Officially registered with Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone Authority (RAKEZ) with three active licences — E-Commerce, Commercial, and General Trading.

UAE & Pakistan Reach

Our main operations run from the UAE with nationwide delivery coverage. We also serve customers across all major cities in Pakistan.

New & Pre-Owned

We carry the latest flagship devices fresh from manufacturers alongside carefully graded certified pre-owned phones at competitive prices.

Direct WhatsApp Orders

No complicated checkout. Simply WhatsApp us your requirement and we'll handle everything from sourcing to delivery with full transparency.

All Major Brands

Customer-First Approach
